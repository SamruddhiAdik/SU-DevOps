# Business Problem and Justification

## Core problem statement
Library members experience operational delays in locating, reserving, borrowing, and returning literary resources due to inefficient manual tracking. Administrative staff lack centralized, real-time visibility, leading to unrecorded losses, reporting errors, and poor user satisfaction.

## Current challenges
- Physical searching through card catalogs is slow.
- Paper checkout ledgers require repeated manual entry.
- Overdue items must be tracked manually.
- Inventory counts can become inconsistent.
- Administrative reports require time-consuming compilation.

## Proposed solution
A centralized Library Management Platform with an indexed catalog, circulation records, due-date tracking, fine calculation, notifications, and administrative reporting.

## Expected business outcomes
The source assignment describes target metrics such as reducing search time from 25 minutes to under 5 seconds, improving queue processing speed by 80%, reducing overdue returns by 40%, and reaching 99.9% record accuracy. These are target outcomes from the assignment, not measured results from this starter project.

## Justification
A shared, searchable source of truth can reduce repeated manual work, improve inventory visibility, and provide more consistent records for librarians and administrators.
